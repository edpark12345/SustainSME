Step 3 · Scope 1 · data review

Scope 1 — direct emissions: your data

The activity data we read from your uploads — on-site fuel, vehicles and refrigerant top-ups. Review and confirm it here; the emissions (tCO₂e) are calculated on the final step.

◐
Measured where you have it, estimated where you don't

Each line is tagged primary when it comes from a bill, meter or log — or estimated when a figure is missing and we model it transparently. Examples: refrigerant leakage = installed charge × an annual leak-rate when no top-up was recorded; heating/electricity from floor area × a benchmark when no bill exists; fuel from vehicle count when litres are unknown. You always see which tier applies.
